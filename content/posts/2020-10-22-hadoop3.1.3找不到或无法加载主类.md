## 找不到或无法加载主类 org.apache.hadoop.mapreduce.v2.app.MRAppMaster

```
> hadoop classpath
xxxxx
```

将hadoop classpath复制到yarn-site.xml

```
  <property>
         <name>yarn.application.classpath</name>
         <value>hadoop classpath</value>
   </property>
```

重启yarn
